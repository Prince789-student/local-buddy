# 🏪 Local Buddy — Neighborhood Essentials Mart & Verified Trade Services

Local Buddy is a hyperlocal digital commerce platform designed for neighborhood retail ecosystems, kirana stores, and local trade experts. It features a complete Merchant ERP with barcode and voice stock entry, an intuitive customer ordering storefront with instant WhatsApp integration, and a verified neighborhood trade experts directory.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **📊 Merchant Hub & ERP**: Manage inventory, monitor real-time stock levels, track revenue, and restock alerts.
- **🎙️ Voice & 📷 Barcode AI Stock Assistant**: Add inventory items via camera barcode scanning or voice commands with multilingual recognition.
- **🛍️ Customer Storefront**: Clean, mobile-first ordering experience with one-tap cart and direct WhatsApp ordering.
- **🛠️ Verified Local Pros**: Directory of verified neighborhood electricians, plumbers, appliance technicians, and carpenters.
- **📑 Interactive Investor Pitch Deck**: Built-in 8-slide presentation (`pitch_deck.html`) and downloadable PDF (`Local_Buddy_Pitch_Deck.pdf`).
